# PetFinder final project: method notes (overview)

These files describe every method used or tried for the Kaggle competition
`deep-learning-for-computer-vision-and-nlp-2026-10` (PetFinder.my adoption speed), why it was done that way,
and what the numbers were. They are written so another agent can look for alternative solutions.

## Task and data
- Train: 6,431 pets, test: 1,891 pets. Columns: `PetID`, `Description`, `AdoptionSpeed` (train only).
- Target classes 1, 2, 3, 4 (no class 0). Train shares: 18.6%, 27.6%, 20.6%, 33.2%.
- Photos: `data/images/images/{train,test}/<PetID>-<n>.jpg`, 37,831 photos belong to listed pets (89 orphan files ignored).
  Photo 1 is the main photo. Mean photos per pet: train 4.43, test 4.95.
- No tabular data (no age, breed, rescuer, fee, etc.), so everything comes from photos and text.
- Metric: quadratic weighted kappa (QWK). Grading in the course: QWK >= 0.5 gives full marks; the rest is for the leaderboard.

## Constraints we kept
- No AutoML libraries (course rule).
- No use of the public original PetFinder.my competition data: it contains these same pets with labels, so it would leak test answers.
- Every Kaggle submission has its own folder `submissions/NN_*` with the executed notebook and the `submission.csv` it wrote.
- Notebooks are generated by `build_notebook.py --version NN` (config dict `VERSIONS` at the top).

## Validation protocol (same everywhere)
- `StratifiedKFold(5, shuffle=True, random_state=42)` on train (version 16 uses 10 folds).
- Three CV numbers are reported:
  - tuned: QWK after cut points tuned on all OOF predictions (optimistic);
  - nested: cut points tuned on 4 folds, applied to the 5th (honest version of "tuned");
  - dist (train-share cuts): in each fold, cut points are quantiles of that fold's predictions at the train class shares.
    This mirrors how test predictions are cut and tracked the leaderboard best.

Top of the public LB at the time: 0.8265, 0.8232, then us at 0.8102.

## Key observations
- LB is about CV + 0.17 to 0.18; LB moves roughly one-to-one with dist CV.
- Public LB noise: equally good models (test predictions correlated 0.994+) scored 0.8060 to 0.8102.
- CV plateau: around 0.627 tuned / 0.625 dist, whatever was added.
